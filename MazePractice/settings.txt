The higher the number for the speed of the player, the faster the player moves,
and the smaller the pixel size, the more pixels can fit on the board meaning that your maze will be larger
The cheats allows you to see the path from you to the end and allows you to see a colored diagram of the maze showing which points are the farthest away from the start.

Controls:
WASD to control character which is blue

If cheats are enabled,
'r' - shows you the solution of the maze
'z' - shows you the distances certain parts of the maze are away from you (Darker = farther away except for pink which is the farthest away)

When the maze is loading you can watch it being made, and after it is made there will be a loading
period where you will be looking at a gray screen, just give the maze some time to load the color map.
If you don't want the color map turn on fast load.
